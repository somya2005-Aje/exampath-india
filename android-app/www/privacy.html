Privacy Policy — ExamPath India

Last updated: 21 November 2026

ExamPath India is a free learning website. There is no login, no account and no sign-up.

What we store

Your saved papers, bookmarks, test history, planner tasks and preferences are stored only in your own browser's local storage, on your device. They are never sent to any server, because this website has no server-side account system and no backend that receives your data.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, password or payment details. We do not run ads that track you across sites in this version.

External links

Links to official exam websites (UPSC, SSC, RRB, IBPS, NTA and others) open those websites, which have their own privacy policies.

Your control

You can delete all locally stored data at any time from the Saved page (“Clear All My Data”) or by clearing your browser's site data.
