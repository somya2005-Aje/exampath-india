Economy 20 Nov 2026

PM inaugurates FICCI centenary AGM

PM Narendra Modi inaugurated FICCI's Annual General Meeting and Annual Convention at Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi, as the chamber marked its centenary. He cited a World Bank report that about 50 crore Indians came out of poverty in 12–13 years, and noted India remains the fastest-growing major economy at around 7% growth.

Source: PIB / CrackTarget, 20 Nov 2026

Economy 19 Nov 2026

FTAs now cover ~US$ 70 trillion of global GDP

Commerce Minister Piyush Goyal said India's preferential market access through FTAs has grown from economies worth ~US$ 10 trillion a few years ago to nearly US$ 70 trillion now.

Source: PIB, 19 Nov 2026

International 18 Nov 2026

India at ASEAN-India & East Asia Summits, Manila

At the 23rd ASEAN-India Summit and 21st East Asia Summit in Manila, India announced initiatives on maritime security, seafarers' safety, cybersecurity and critical minerals under its Act East Policy.

Source: Vajiram & Ravi, 18 Nov 2026

Economy 07 Nov 2026

Forex reserves at US$ 717.056 billion

India's foreign exchange reserves stood at US$ 717.056 billion on 30 October 2026, down US$ 2.691 billion in a week.

Source: ExamAtlas, 7 Nov 2026

Science & Tech 06 Nov 2026

Semicon 2.0 portal launched

Union Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw launched the Semicon 2.0 Web Application Portal, opening applications under Pillars 2, 3 and 4 of the India Semiconductor Mission (Cabinet outlay ₹1,27,500 crore). MeitY also cleared 12 electronics projects worth ₹7,503 crore.

Source: ExamAtlas, 7 Nov 2026

International 06 Nov 2026

Bahamas becomes 116th ISA member

The Bahamas deposited its Instrument of Accession to the International Solar Alliance at the 9th ISA Assembly session in New Delhi, taking membership to 116.

Source: ExamAtlas, 7 Nov 2026

Governance 06 Nov 2026

Digital Life Certificate Campaign 5

Dr Jitendra Singh launched the 5th nationwide DLC campaign; nearly 40 lakh digital life certificates were recorded in the first five days, with Aadhaar face authentication rising from 61% to nearly 80%.

Source: ExamAtlas, 7 Nov 2026

Economy 04 Nov 2026

Andhra Pradesh tops vehicle ownership ease index

Andhra Pradesh topped the Ease of Vehicle Ownership Index (EVOI) with 5.91/10, followed by Kerala (5.74) and Tamil Nadu (4.58). The index covered 15 states accounting for over 85% of India's organised used-car market.

Source: Indian Express UPSC Essentials, Nov 2026

Environment Nov 2026

CBD COP 17 adopts Yerevan Pathway

CBD COP 17 was held 19–30 October 2026 in Yerevan, Armenia, under the theme 'Taking Action for Nature'. Parties adopted the Yerevan Pathway and rules for the new Article 8(j) subsidiary body (Sevan Guidelines). COP 18 will be held in Ecuador in 2028.

Source: Indian Express UPSC Essentials, Nov 2026

Art & Culture Nov 2026

Indian epics in UNESCO Memory of the World Register

Manuscripts of the Mahabharata, Rajatarangini and Arthashastra earned a place in UNESCO's Memory of the World Asia-Pacific Regional Register.

Source: Indian Express UPSC Essentials, Nov 2026
