CSIR NET

National Testing Agency • All India • Eligibility: M.Sc / Integrated BS-MS (or equivalent) in the relevant science subject.

✓ Official archive
Exam Pattern
Single paper: Part A (General Aptitude) + Part B & C (subject). Negative marking varies by part. Computer-based test, held twice a year (June & December).
