Polity

Indian Constitution — Basics

Adopted 26 Nov 1949, enforced 26 Jan 1950. Longest written constitution in the world. Preamble: Justice, Liberty, Equality, Fraternity. Parts: 22 (originally), Articles: 395 (originally), Schedules: 12. Dr. B.R. Ambedkar chaired the Drafting Committee. Fundamental Rights: Articles 12–35 (Part III). DPSP: Articles 36–51 (Part IV).

Polity

Fundamental Rights (Part III)

Right to Equality (14–18), Right to Freedom (19–22), Right against Exploitation (23–24), Right to Freedom of Religion (25–28), Cultural & Educational Rights (29–30), Right to Constitutional Remedies (32). Article 32 — Dr. Ambedkar called it the 'heart and soul' of the Constitution.

Geography

Rivers of India — Quick Facts

Ganga (2,525 km) — longest river in India; originates at Gangotri (Uttarkashi). Brahmaputra (2,900 km total) — originates in Tibet as Tsangpo. Godavari (1,465 km) — longest peninsular river, 'Dakshin Ganga'. Narmada — flows west into the Arabian Sea. Indus — originates near Mansarovar.

Economy

Indian Economy — Key Institutions

RBI (1935) — central bank, issues currency, repo rate setter. SEBI (1992) — capital markets regulator. NITI Aayog (2015) — replaced Planning Commission. GST (1 July 2017) — 'One Nation, One Tax'. Fiscal deficit target and FRBM Act are favourite exam topics.

History

Modern History — Key Dates

1857 — First War of Independence. 1885 — Indian National Congress founded. 1905 — Partition of Bengal. 1919 — Jallianwala Bagh. 1920 — Non-Cooperation Movement. 1930 — Dandi March. 1942 — Quit India Movement. 1947 — Independence (15 August).

Science

Science — Everyday Facts

Speed of light: 3×10⁸ m/s. Boiling point of water: 100°C. Human body: 206 bones (adult), 4 heart chambers. Photosynthesis: 6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ (in chlorophyll, sunlight). SI units: metre (length), kilogram (mass), second (time), ampere (current), kelvin (temperature).

Maths

Maths Shortcuts — Percentage & Ratio

x% of y = y% of x. To find % change: (Change ÷ Original) × 100. Ratio a:b — if a+b given, a = (a/(a+b)) × total. Simple Interest = PRT/100. Speed = Distance ÷ Time; km/h → m/s multiply by 5/18. Average = Sum ÷ Count.

Strategy

Current Affairs — How to Revise

Daily: 10 CA facts + 1 quiz. Weekly: revise the week's facts (Sunday). Monthly: one-page summary per topic (Polity, Economy, Environment, Science). Always note the SOURCE and DATE of every fact — UPSC/SSC ask 'which/when' questions, not opinions.
